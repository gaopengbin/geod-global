# Nighttime-light public-original research — 2026-10-12

This investigation checks numerical originals, not rendered night maps. It uses no new account, credential, authentication bypass or Earth Engine computation. Exa reviewed 10 search results across two source-discovery questions; the conclusions below also use actual publisher HTTP responses, unchanged original metadata, complete original transfers and independent Rasterio reads. A catalog entry or a TIFF header alone is not native acceptance.

## Author-published 2024 alternative

The author record is [Hengl v0.4, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17294744](https://zenodo.org/records/17294744). The accessible [original record export](https://zenodo.org/records/17294744/export/json) and [official file list](https://zenodo.org/api/records/17294744/files) were retrieved as raw JSON and retained without changes. The record is published, version v0.4, with CC BY 4.0 rights. It describes VIIRS annual VNL-derived processed years 2012–2024 and backward logistic-model extrapolation for 2000–2011. Earlier model years are not observation years and are excluded from a proposed connector. This is the author's processed distribution, not an unmodified EOG original or NASA Black Marble.

The exact 2024 file entry declares:

- File: `nightlights.average_viirs.v21_m_500m_s_20240101_20241231_go_epsg4326_v20250904.tif`.
- Size: 63,713,625 bytes; original MD5 `0b976892d40c2d0d99177c727289eaf2`.
- Author's canonical [API content URL](https://zenodo.org/api/records/17294744/files/nightlights.average_viirs.v21_m_500m_s_20240101_20241231_go_epsg4326_v20250904.tif/content); [human-facing file URL](https://zenodo.org/records/17294744/files/nightlights.average_viirs.v21_m_500m_s_20240101_20241231_go_epsg4326_v20250904.tif).
- File entry metadata: 86,400 × 35,849. Hidden false, transfer completed in the published repository. Those repository status fields do not certify this machine's transfer completed.

Initial record/API and `?download=1` attempts returned 504 or timed out. The export/file-list endpoints subsequently succeeded, and a human-facing file GET returned TIFF bytes; later complete GET attempts also failed before the canonical API content route started transferring. These are real transient route failures and remain in the private receipts. They are not authentication failures and were not worked around with an account or an unlisted mirror.

The canonical API content whole-file GET returned HTTP 200 and declared 63,713,625 bytes, then closed after 19,497,168 received bytes. Only 18,874,368 complete 1 MiB chunks were written to the retained partial file; that failed attempt has no complete-file checksum. The response declared the duplicate MIME value `image/tiff, image/tiff` and no ETag. Recovery used exact public byte ranges from the same formal versioned URL, without authentication or a mirror. One sequential 4 MiB range completed after a timeout; the next unfinished sequential request was stopped before it appended bytes. The resulting 23,068,672-byte prefix was retained with its hash. Ten subsequent ranges covered every remaining byte, using at most three simultaneous requests; temporary 502/503 responses and a timed-out missing range were retained, then that missing range succeeded on a further retry. Every accepted segment required HTTP 206 and its exact `Content-Range`, declared total and byte count.

The final assembled reference is exactly **63,713,625 bytes**, with publisher-matching MD5 **`0b976892d40c2d0d99177c727289eaf2`** and independently calculated SHA-256 **`e6edde68952052f8c53050e2ce7082bab08ddd0e15a696f999215e2d57194c53`**. Its complete receipt links all earlier whole-GET failures and Range attempts. This proves a byte-identical independent reference, not one uninterrupted whole-file GET, dependable native recovery or a native download receipt. Phase timings overlap and include different retries, so no misleading single throughput or whole-download duration is calculated from their sum.

Independent Rasterio decoding read all **3,097,353,600 original pixels** in 24.735 seconds: signed Int16, one band, EPSG:4326, PixelIsArea, 86,400 × 35,849, 512 × 512 tiled storage and representable scalar NoData -32768. The actual bounds are `[-180, -62.000833333333446, 180.00000000000028, 87.37]`, with 15-arc-second cells. The grid is below the current 8-billion pixel and 131,072 tiled-axis limits. The tiny floating-point east-edge excess is intersected with legal physical longitude for the client query footprint only; original bounds, transform and pixels remain unchanged. Declared nominal 500 m resolution is not constant ground distance at every latitude.

The original mask distinguishes 256,435,200 NoData pixels and 2,840,918,400 non-NoData pixels. Non-NoData values range from -11 to 32767: **3,299** negative samples, **3,032** values above 2000, and **2,765,917,660** original zero samples. The unexplained extreme values are not scientifically certified by being non-NoData. Neither negatives nor 32767 are converted into new masks. Selected DN and masks: Beijing 150/255, Shanghai 64/255, New York 61/255, Atlantic 0/255, Amazon 56/255, Arctic -32768/0 and southern edge 0/255. A zero remains an original non-NoData value and is not equated with zero observations or verified absence of light. The scientific count/quality layers necessary for stronger interpretation were not substituted or invented.

There is an important numerical-declaration conflict: the author's record says the input 0–200 range was converted to 0–2000, implying a nominal inverse factor of 0.1, while the actual TIFF embeds scale **10**, offset 0 and no unit. The original TIFF tag names its input as `VNL_npp_2024_global_vcmslcfg_v2_c202502261200.average_masked.dat.tif`. Neither the scale nor raw values will be rewritten. The proposed product must disclose both declarations and provide the original processed DN; it cannot silently label them calibrated nW/cm²/sr or normalized proportions. Year 2024 is an annual reference bin, not a scene timestamp or the 2025 file publication time.

The author's actual 2,663-byte [processing script](https://zenodo.org/api/records/17294744/files/annual_lights.night_500m.R/content) was retrieved completely. Its MD5 `8205f775b5b165c34bdb7bcb9d429516` matches the file-list declaration. It models 2000–2011 backward from 2012–2024 inputs, using a nominal 2000 normalization and output rules for those modeled years. It does not independently document the complete physical calibration of the separate 2024 delivered file, so it is not used to resolve the contradictory scale in the software.

- Actual original MD5: `6b09c5def35b59e0897106f0f4cf01c5`.
- Actual original SHA-256: `2332fd3e7d3f846915b02d9da964c212279a82b575b63023423a1b66139899a0`.
- Actual MD5 happens to match the older Zenodo v0.2 2021 file declaration. That does not repair the current STAC checksum, identify it with the newer v0.4 file, or justify silently replacing its source record.

Independent full decode read all **3,097,353,600 pixels**: EPSG:4326, Int16, 86,400 × 35,849, tiled, scalar NoData -32768. Non-NoData pixels total 2,840,918,400; NoData pixels total 256,435,200. Raw non-NoData range is -32766 to 32760, with 35,718 negative non-NoData pixels and 30,623 values above 2000. No scientific validity is inferred for unexplained extreme signed values. Zero remains an original non-NoData value; a zero-light interpretation must respect product processing and the unavailable observation-count/quality context.

Selected original values: Beijing 1230, Shanghai 620, New York 780, Atlantic 0 and Amazon 460. These are DN, not directly declared calibrated radiance. The full file remains under `.tif.part` because of the checksum conflict. Its unchanged STAC unit/scale fields are preserved rather than replaced by a guessed physical inverse.

## Other publisher evidence and limits

The [EOG VNL specifications](https://eogdata.mines.edu/products/vnl/) distinguish annual processed light, monthly averages, nightly mosaics and observation-count products. Those products have different filters, masks and periods; missing observations and a numerical zero cannot be conflated. The actual [EOG license PDF](https://eogdata.mines.edu/files/EOG_products_CC_License.pdf) identifies VNL among the CC BY 4.0 products and specifies attribution. The tested delivery routes nevertheless require login. No registration or credential was created.

[NASA's VNP46A4.002 specification](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/laads-vnp46a4-2) identifies yearly lunar/atmospheric corrected nighttime light in HDF5, including quality and observation layers. A genuine [2025 h29v05 granule](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/archive/allData/5200/VNP46A4/2025/001/VNP46A4.A2025001.h29v05.002.2026077002306.h5) returned an Earthdata authorization page rather than HDF bytes. NASA public-use terms do not remove that delivery requirement. No official anonymous numerical COG was established here.
